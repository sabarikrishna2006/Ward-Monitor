AI Deterioration Risk — Case Study Showcase

Three real ICU stays, replayed through the trained early-warning model exactly as it scored them during evaluation. This is a fixed, pre-computed snapshot — not a live feed, and it is not connected to any hospital system or patient database.

Every number on this page comes from the same model run and evaluation pipeline used to measure the deterioration model, on de-identified historical intensive-care records (MIMIC-IV). Nothing here is simulated or hand-picked to look good — only re-formatted for a readable walk-through.
How to read this: the top chart is the patient's NEWS2 clinical score, the same score nurses already chart hourly at the bedside. The bottom chart is the model's own output — a calibrated probability that the patient will cross a serious deterioration threshold within the next 12 hours, recomputed fresh every hour from a rolling window of vitals. The two are shown as separate charts, deliberately never combined into one, because they are different units measuring different things: one is an observed clinical score, the other is a model-estimated probability.
