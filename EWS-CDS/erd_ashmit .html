Foqal CareOS — Unified Database ERD v3.1

Three-schema architecture on Google Cloud SQL PostgreSQL 15+. The admission_id UUID is the universal key flowing from hospital_core into both ews and discharge_ai. staff_id UUID is the universal actor key for all clinical attributions.

hospital_core

Master Data Schema — 16 Tables

Shared schema owned by neither team. Both ews and discharge_ai reference these tables via FK. HOSPITALS is the multi-tenancy root. ADMISSIONS.admission_id (UUID) is the universal key that flows into every downstream table. STAFF replaces all free-text name fields — every clinical action is attributed to a staff UUID for NABH MN-4 compliance.

ews

EWS Schema — Ward Monitor (10 Tables)

Foqal CareOS early warning system. All 10 tables FK to hospital_core.admissions.admission_id (shown as anchor entity). Vitals retain full 12-field richness. Drug-lab rules move to DB with hospital-level scoping. DCM assessments and fluid balance are new tables specific to dilated cardiomyopathy monitoring. The step-down transfer workflow has a multi-stage approval FSM.

discharge_ai

Discharge Summary AI Schema — 22 Tables

Discharge summary AI system. ACTIVE_PATIENTS is the bridge table linking MIMIC-IV hadm_id to hospital_core.admissions.admission_id. The Gemini pipeline, MedCPT embeddings, and all 18 ap_* MIMIC-IV clinical tables are preserved. Section-level NABH tracking with a doctor amendment FSM ensures each of the 15 NABH discharge sections can be individually rejected and regenerated. The 3-tier AI error log (tier 1 minor, tier 2 factual, tier 3 hallucination) is unique to this architecture.
