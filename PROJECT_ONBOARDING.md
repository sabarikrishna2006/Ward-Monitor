# Foqal CareOS: Early Warning System (EWS-CDS) - Project Onboarding

Welcome to the project! This document serves as a quick-start guide for new developers and researchers joining the team to help you understand the foundational data, research, and architecture behind our clinical deterioration prediction system.

---

## 1. Datasets Used

Our project heavily relies on real-world electronic health record (EHR) data to simulate, train, and test our machine learning models and clinical decision support rules.

*   **MIMIC-IV (Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care)**
    *   **Description:** A large, freely-available database comprising de-identified health-related data associated with over forty thousand patients who stayed in critical care units of the Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center.
    *   **Link:** [MIMIC-IV on PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/)
    *   **Usage in Project:** The `ap_*` (Analytics Project) tables are modeled directly on the MIMIC-IV schema (e.g., `ap_chartevents`, `ap_labevents`) to ensure our system works seamlessly with industry-standard retrospective data formats.

---

## 2. Key Research & Clinical Literature

The logic of our Early Warning System is backed by established clinical protocols and recent advances in machine learning for patient deterioration.

*   **Clinical Foundation: NEWS2 (National Early Warning Score 2)**
    *   **Paper/Resource:** Royal College of Physicians (UK). *National Early Warning Score (NEWS) 2: Standardising the assessment of acute-illness severity in the NHS*.
    *   **Link:** [RCP NEWS2 Official Guidance](https://www.rcplondon.ac.uk/projects/outputs/national-early-warning-score-news-2)
    *   **Usage:** Our deterministic rule-based engine calculates a NEWS2 score based on live patient vitals to dictate immediate clinical escalation and monitoring frequencies (e.g., Ward vs. CCU).

*   **Machine Learning Foundation: Predicting Clinical Deterioration**
    *   **Paper/Resource:** Churpek et al. (2016). *Multicenter Comparison of Machine Learning Methods and Early Warning Scores for Predicting Clinical Deterioration in Wards*.
    *   **Link:** [PubMed / Critical Care Medicine](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26646458/)
    *   **Usage:** We run an independent ML model that analyzes subtle trends in `ews_vitals_timeseries` to predict high deterioration risk up to 9 hours before NEWS2 reaches a critical threshold. This serves as our "Non-Vacuous AI Alert" panel.

## 3. Database Architecture (Common DB)

We share a single Postgres database instance (Common DB) to ensure seamless integration between the hospital management side and the clinical decision support (CDS) side. 

### Core Application Tables (Shared Hub)
*   `active_patients`: The master hub of all currently admitted patients. All EWS and analytics tables link back to `hadm_id` and `subject_id` here.
*   `app_users`: Hospital staff, nurses, and doctors.
*   `app_encounters`, `app_summaries`, `app_audit_log`: General hospital operations tracking.

### Analytics Data (Ashmit's Scope)
These tables mirror the MIMIC-IV structure and are primarily used for batch data, research analytics, and billing pipelines.
*   **Vitals & Labs:** `ap_chartevents`, `ap_labevents`, `ap_microbiologyevents`
*   **Admin & Flow:** `ap_admissions`, `ap_transfers`, `ap_icustays`, `ap_services`
*   **Diagnoses & Billing:** `ap_diagnoses`, `ap_procedures`, `ap_drgcodes`
*   **Medications:** `ap_prescriptions`, `ap_pharmacy`, `ap_poe`, `ap_omr`

### Early Warning CDS Data (Sabari's Scope)
These tables contain real-time streaming data for the active patient monitoring dashboards (Ward Monitor) and ML alerting.
*   `ews_vitals_timeseries`: Real-time streaming vitals (SpO2, RR, HR, BP, Temp, AVPU) used for live NEWS2 calculation.
*   `ews_lab_events`: Recent lab pulls (Potassium, Creatinine, Lactate) used for drug-lab contraindication rules.
*   `ews_medications`: Active patient prescriptions relevant to EWS rules (e.g., ACE inhibitors, Potassium-sparing diuretics).
*   `ews_escalations`: Tracking triggered NEWS2 and clinical alerts.
*   `ews_ai_alert_ack`: Tracking nurse acknowledgements and dismissals of AI-generated deterioration alerts to prevent alert fatigue.
*   `ews_ccu_transfers`: Step-up and Step-down transfer queues between General Ward and CCU.
*   `ews_drug_lab_actions`: Recommendations generated by our rules engine (e.g., Stop NSAIDs if Creatinine > 1.5).
